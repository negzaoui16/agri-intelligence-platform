8 rules: think like a farmer, not an engineer

The science stays the same. Only the words and the order change.

1. One question per screen The title is the farmer's question: "Do I have enough water?", not "Water Sources, FAO-56 & Pump Hydraulics".
2. Answer first, numbers second "Not in July." Then 196 vs 621 m³. Formulas and data sources go behind "How do we know this?".
3. Every problem comes with a fix Never show a red number alone. Always add "What can you do?" with 2–3 choices.
4. Be honest on the home screen The biggest problem goes on top. No "100%" or "Highly feasible" when the pump only gives a third of July's water.
5. Word + icon + color, never color alone "Good", "OK", "Problem" are always written. Colors differ in lightness too, for color-blind users.
6. Big enough for the field Text 16px or bigger, buttons 48–60px tall, high contrast. Works in bright sun and with dirty fingers.
7. "Skip if you don't know" Pump efficiency, pipe size, tilt: optional. We use a safe estimate and say so. Adding a farm takes 3 steps, not 20 fields.
8. Speak, listen, own language Arabic (right to left), French, English. Microphone to ask, "Listen" to hear answers. Only 4 tabs: Home, My farm, Ask, Report.
