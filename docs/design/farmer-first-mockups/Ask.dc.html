How many solar panels do I need for my pump?

About 24 panels for most of the year. About 40 panels for July.

You have 12 kW now (24 panels of 500 W). That is more than enough over the whole year. In July the trees need more water, so the sun covers only 6 of every 10 units.

Cheaper choice: keep 24 panels and add a water tank for summer.

How do we know this? Sun data for your exact location from the European solar model, plus your pump settings. This is an estimate, not a quote.
