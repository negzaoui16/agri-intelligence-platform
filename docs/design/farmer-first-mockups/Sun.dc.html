Sun power

Can the sun run my pump?

Yes, most of the year.

With your 12 kW of panels (about 24 panels), over a full year:

Sun makes18,816 kWh
Pump uses10,930 kWh

But not enough in July.

Trees drink the most when it is hot. The sun gives 6 of every 10 units your pump needs.

How to cover summer

  • Store water in a tankPump on sunny days, water later. Tank size: about 931 m³.
  • Keep the grid or a generatorOnly for the hot months.
